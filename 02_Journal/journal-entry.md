## Reflection
As someone that can fight fires, save lives, and work on every peice of electronics on earth I find myself half lost in the woods with this new to me research methods. On one hand I know a lot of the basic communication methods but never really had the vocabulary, or framwork to employ the theries.
Defining terms and establishing the tools needed to colect, ingest, organize, and insure that the information isn't simply common knowledge. Finding data sources is onle a small part of media research, but all sources are not the same. Partly looking at one source that was good enough to cite sources they used can help in the creation of the lititure review so that the final result of the research will add to the existing knowledge base and not just repeat whaat has been done.
