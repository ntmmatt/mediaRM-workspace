     I had never heard of this concept but I had lived it without knowing what was happening or that there were words for it. For example, in diagnosing anything from plumbing to humans you have a set guideline to follow, but that doesn't stop thoughts like, "I knew that," or "I would have seen that." Mine have changed over the years to be more like, "If this test is good, then what?" or "That's new." I think where some of the postdiction may come from is that it's so natural for people we named it in several circles. For example, "hindsight is 20/20," or "Monday morning quarterback," or "I could have told you that."
